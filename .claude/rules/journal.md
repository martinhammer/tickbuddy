# Journal screen

- **Journal** (`JournalView.vue`, `DaysDownGrid.vue`, `TracksDownGrid.vue`, `JournalCell.vue`): two layouts, Days down (rows of days, newest first) and Tracks down (rows of tracks, days across, today first). The layout button shows MDI's `phone-rotate-landscape` in Days down (the portrait phone is the current layout, the arrow points to the other) and `phone-rotate-portrait` in Tracks down. Both scroll inside one container under a fixed toolbar, with a sticky header row and first column, and load 30 more days as the end comes into view. A cell reads like the old View journal at rest and shows its controls under `:hover` or keyboard focus (`:has(:focus-visible)`, not `:focus-within`, so the bar doesn't stay open after a click moves the pointer away) in one floating bar with a shadow, vertically centred on the row and placed from the cell's centre (not centred on the cell), so the value never moves: the checkbox where the ✓ sits, then a divider and the note button (Today's size, 32px with a 20px icon); a counter's number where it sits, "−" and "+" about 30px either side, then the divider and note button. The bar may cover neighbouring cells. On `@media (hover: none)` the same arrangement always shows, in the cell rather than floating, without the note button. A Number or Choice cell there is tapped on the bar's own control, as a click on a desktop lands, never on the at-rest value (`.restValue`, hidden): the Number's field sits in the cell looking like the value (centred, no ring or background until focused, placeholder hidden, 2px side padding so the widest number fits a column `--cell-label-width` wide, as core's `text-overflow: ellipsis` on inputs would otherwise cut it, unit hidden), and a tap focuses it natively; the Choice's button is drawn borderless, centred, the column's width and blank when empty (`.emptyText`), and a tap mounts and opens the drop-down; a note's dot follows the control (`.dotInBar`). An earlier at-rest button that swapped itself for the field after a tap never opened it on a phone, and had no size on an empty day. Controls stay in the DOM so Tab reaches them. A Yes/No cell's native `input.checkbox` is kept inside its slot (`inset 0`, invisible) rather than where core hides it, 10000px to the left: a tap on its label focuses it, and the grid scrolled back to its first column to show it. A date (or day header) opens Today on that day; a track name opens its Analytics. "Go to date" (an `NcPopover` with `NcDateTimePickerNative`) makes the chosen day the first one. A "Today" button shows whenever today is out of view, after Go to date or after scrolling today's row or column under the sticky header row or name column (measured on scroll against the `data-sticky` corner and the `data-today` row/column, not an `IntersectionObserver`, which can't see what a sticky cell covers), and comes back. Cells talk to the view through `JournalActions` (`src/journal.ts`).
