# Today screen

- **Today** (`TodayView.vue`, `TodayRow.vue`): a left-aligned column (660px of rows at most), indented past the navigation toggle as a whole, as the Tracks page is, so rows start under the title; under 1024px only the header is, and the rows use the full width with 12px side padding, and the header sticks to the top (`position: sticky`, on the page's background, taking the column's top padding as its own) so the navigation toggle always sits beside the title and the rows scroll under it; one unboxed row per visible track for one day, a line under each. A row is the name block, then the controls, 8px between them. The controls sit in two columns shared by every row through CSS subgrid (the list `.rows` is a three-column grid, each row and its control area a subgrid; gaps live only on `.rows`, as a subgrid's own gap turns into extra margin): the **value column** (`--today-value-width`), whose contents end on one right edge down the page (a counter's round tertiary − and its number, the checkbox in a 3ch slot centred on the number, a Number's field and a Choice's drop-down at the column's full width), then the **last column**, start-aligned, holding a counter's +, a Number's unit (cut at 8ch, full text in its `title`; under 1024px the unit moves inside the field instead, see Track types in `.claude/rules/frontend.md`) or nothing, as wide as the + or the widest unit on screen (`minmax(clickable area, auto)`). With only Yes/No and Counter on screen the value column is the clickable area plus 3ch and there is no gap, so "− 0 +" and the checkbox sit as they always did; once a Number or Choice is on screen (`wideControls`, `.rowsWide`) it is 140px, on phones too, with a 6px gap. A Number's field there is the drop-down's visible box, not the column: NcSelect draws its box 2px inside its own edges (measured 136 × 30 in the 140px column), so `.valueNumber` takes `--select-inset: 2px` off each side of the field's width and height and adds it as end padding, and the two line up on all four edges. "Not supported yet" stays in the value column (`nowrap`), so it never widens the last column. The name block has two lines at every width: the name, wrapping rather than cut, with the note icon right after it (laid out as text, `.name` inline, so the icon follows the last word of a wrapped name), then the last seven days as squares (in their own `.squares` span) and the run, which sits beside them where it fits and wraps onto the line under them, at its start, where it doesn't (`.week` shrinks and wraps; `column-gap: 9px` is the squares' 3px gap plus the run's old 6px margin) (`src/runText.ts`, Tickcli's wording, in `--color-text-maxcontrast` for streaks and breaks alike) — the squares follow the day shown (`squaresEnd` in `dates.ts`): the seven days ending today, or the day shown and the six after it once it is more than six days back, newest on the left, the day shown outlined (dashed while open, solid once ticked), so a step back moves the outline or scrolls the squares by one day, never jumps; the run **always shows the real today**; the squares and the run together are one link to `#analytics/<id>` (a single Tab stop, `aria-label` and the run's `title` "Show analytics for <name>", the squares keeping their per-date titles, no underline or hover styling, on locked days too). A row without a note is those two lines: its note icon shows only under `:hover`, `:focus-within` and always on `@media (hover: none)`, hidden with opacity so its space is kept (the squares never move) and Tab reaches it. A row with a note has a third line: the note icon, then the note; the icon after the name is replaced by an empty space of the same size, also on a locked day. A click on the track name opens the note field too, on any row (a mouse convenience; the icon is the keyboard and screen-reader way in), as do the note line's icon and text; the open field appears as that third line, and saving it empty removes it. The controls stay centred on the row's height. A locked day has no icon after the name and no click on the name, shows a note read-only with a static icon, and shows its values where the controls would be, so nothing moves when a day locks: the ✓ in the checkbox's slot, the count where the counter's number is (no −, no +), a Number's value (`formatNumber`, without the unit) ending where the field's text ends (the field's 8px padding) with its unit in the last column (under 1024px, with the unit after it, `numberText`, as the field shows it), and a Choice's label left-aligned in a box the value column's width, where the drop-down draws it (NcSelect's label padding). The header holds the eye, the Editable days button (only while the day shown is locked, or on a day just unlocked with it, until another day is shown), the "Today" button, then the day arrows. The title follows how far back the day is (`dayHeading` in `dates.ts`: "Today", "Yesterday", "2 days ago" to "6 days ago", then the date), and a "Today" button sits before the day arrows while another day is shown. The note icon, here and in the Journal, is MDI's `note-text-outline`, not a pencil: the pencil (pencil-outline) means rename only (the sidebar title and Rename track, as in Tickdroid). At rest it is lighter than the run text, `--color-text-maxcontrast` mixed 75% with the background (3:1 against it, WCAG's minimum for icons); the text colour under the pointer or with keyboard focus. Loads tracks, the last seven days' ticks (plus the squares' seven days when the day shown is further back), today's stats and the day's notes.
